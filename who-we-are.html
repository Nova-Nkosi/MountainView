WHO WE ARE

A boutique firm that knows your name.

MountainView Group is a boutique full-service accounting firm in South Africa, primarily serving small to medium enterprises and NGOs.

What boutique means

Personal service. You don't get passed around. You get a team that understands small business and NGO realities — tight budgets, funder deadlines, SARS pressure, and the need for clear numbers.

What we believe

Good accounting is not just compliance. It's clarity. When your numbers are clean, you can plan, apply for funding, win tenders, and grow with confidence.

Our Team

MountainView Group is run by a dedicated team. When you work with us, you work with a firm — with systems, checks and support — not a single person juggling everything.
